# How to fill in the labelling sheet

Open each posting's URL, read it, and write the option id you judge correct in that
question's column. Leave a cell empty when the posting genuinely does not say: those are
excluded from the score rather than counted against the model.

Do not look at what the model answered first. A label that agrees because it was suggested
measures nothing.

## arrangement

Whether the work is remote, hybrid, or on site.

- `remote`: The role can be done fully remotely, possibly limited to stated countries or time zones
- `hybrid`: Regular office days are required, and some remote work is allowed
- `onsite`: The work happens at an office or other site, with no remote option stated
- `not_stated`: The posting does not say where the work is done

## seniority

How senior the role is, from intern to leadership.

- `0`: Internship, apprenticeship, or working student
- `1`: Entry level: new graduates or early career, little experience expected
- `2`: Mid level: works independently on well-defined problems
- `3`: Senior: leads projects, mentors others, owns ambiguous problems
- `4`: Staff, principal, or leadership: sets direction across teams or manages managers

## sponsorship

Whether visa sponsorship is offered, refused, or unmentioned.

- `offers`: States that visa sponsorship is available for this role
- `does_not_offer`: States that sponsorship is not available, or that candidates must already be authorized to work
- `not_mentioned`: Says nothing about visa sponsorship or work authorization

## onCall

Whether the role includes an on-call rota.

- `no`: No
- `yes`: Yes
